## WHO IS KṚṢṆA?

![]()

**Before we ask when Kṛṣṇa appeared, perhaps we should ask a deeper question:**

### **Who appeared?**

We remember Janmāṣṭamī as the night Kṛṣṇa appeared in Mathurā.

But the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam presents something deeper.

Kṛṣṇa is described as **the Unborn who appears**.

At midnight, in the darkness of the prison, He appears—

**“like the full moon rising in the eastern sky.”**
*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.3.8*

The Sanskrit word is **āvirāsīt — appeared, manifested.**

And so the mystery begins:

### \*\*The Unborn appeared.

The Infinite became visible.
The Supreme became a child.\*\*

**Who, then, is the One who appeared?**
