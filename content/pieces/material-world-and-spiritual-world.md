In Gaudiya Vaishnava cosmology (like the teachings found in Chaitanya Charitamrita and ISKCON), the exact percentages are actually flipped: the entire material world is 25 percent (one-fourth) of creation, while the spiritual world is 75 percent (three-fourths). [1, 2]

The concept you are referring to maps out the distinct realms of existence based on the moods of Aishwarya (opulence/reverence) and Madhurya (sweetness/intimate love).

## 1. The Material World (Devi-dhama / Brahmanda) — 25%

According to the Purusha Suktam ("Ekapaad_asya_iha_abhavat_punah"), only one-quarter of God's energy manifests as the material universes.

This is where souls experience the cycle of birth and death. [1, 3, 4]

## 2. The Spiritual Sky (Paravyoma / Vaikuntha) — 75%

The remaining three-quarters of reality is completely spiritual, eternal, and free from anxiety (Vaikuntha means "without anxiety"). Within this massive 75% spiritual realm, there is a clear distinction in how God is worshipped: [3, 5, 6]

## The Core Difference: Aishwarya vs. Go Lok

In the Vaikuntha planets (Aishwarya realm): You are fully aware that God is the Supreme Almighty Lord. You bow down, maintain distance, and offer majestic prayers. [7, 8, 9]

In Goloka (The topmost planet of Vaikuntha): The majesty (Aishwarya) of God is completely hidden by Yogamaya (divine energy). Krishna's friends wrestle with him, his mother scolds him, and the Gopis dance with him. Intimate sweetness replaces awe and reverence. [7, 8, 10]

So, while the material world is the 25%, the 75% spiritual world holds both the vast majestic planets of Vaikuntha (Aishwarya) and the ultimate crowning jewel, Goloka. [10, 11, 12, 13]

In the Srimad Bhagavatam (Canto 3, Chapter 15) and various Vaishnava texts, the Vaikuntha planets are described as the absolute Kingdom of God—an eternal realm made entirely of Sat-Chit-Ananda (eternity, absolute knowledge, and pure bliss). Because it is a purely spiritual dimension, it lacks the physical limitations, suffering, and decay found in our material world. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

The specific features of what exists on the Vaikuntha planets include the following details:

## 1. The Inhabitants Look Like the Lord

Four-Armed Forms: The resident devotees of Vaikuntha possess a sky-bluish complexion and four arms. They carry the same divine symbols as Lord Narayana: the conch shell (Shankha), discus (Chakra), mace (Gada), and lotus flower (Padma). [2, 6]

Eternal Youth: There is no old age, disease, birth, or death. Everyone is perpetually in the prime of youth and possesses a highly attractive, effulgent appearance. [3, 6]

No Material Lust: Men and women interact with absolute spiritual affection. Although they are surrounded by stunning celestial beauty, the inhabitants are never stimulated by material or physical passion; their singular pleasure is chanting the glories of the Lord. [7, 8, 9]

## 2. The Surrounding Nature and Architecture

Self-Luminous Abode: There are no suns, moons, or electricity in Vaikuntha. The entire spiritual sky is naturally illuminated by the dazzling bodily effulgence (Brahmajoti) emanating from the Lord.

Wish-Fulfilling Trees (Kalpavriksha): The forests are filled with desire trees that yield any flower or fruit requested, regardless of the season.

Transcendent Wildlife: The lakes feature crystal-clear, nectar-like water. The local animals, such as deer, pigeons, and cuckoos, actively pause their own activities to listen to the musical praises dedicated to Vishnu.

Palaces and Spiritual Airplanes: The landscapes are adorned with golden palaces, hanging gardens, and walls made of marble and gold borders. Inhabitants travel between these unlimited spiritual planets using beautifully constructed spiritual airplanes made of lapis lazuli, emeralds, and gold. [3, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14]

## 3. The Divine Presences

Different Incarnations: Vaikuntha is not just one single planet, but an infinite cluster of spiritual worlds. Each planet is presided over by a distinct expansion or incarnation of the Lord (such as Lord Narayana, Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, and Narasimha) alongside their respective pure devotees. [12, 14, 15]

Specific Holy Realms: Embedded within this spiritual sky are monumental abodes like Ayodhya/Saketa Loka (where Lord Rama resides), Dwarka Loka (where Krishna rules as a majestic king), and at the topmost summit, the crown jewel planet of Goloka Vrindavan. [11, 16, 17]

Goddess Lakshmi’s Service: Goddess Lakshmi, along with her millions of expansions, constantly serves the Lord. They can be observed tending to Tulsi gardens, cleaning the emerald pathways, or playing musical instruments to entertain the Supreme Lord. [8, 12, 13]

To clarify, Mahavishnu does not reside inside the spiritual Vaikuntha planets. Instead, He sits right on the boundary between the spiritual and material realms, while His personal expansions reside inside the realms. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

According to the Satvata-tantra and Vaishnava cosmogony, Lord Narayana is the supreme master who resides eternally inside the spiritual Vaikuntha planets. To create the material world, a specific chain of cosmic expansions (Purusha Avatars) takes place: [1, 2, 6, 7, 8]

## 1. Mahavishnu (Karanodakasayi Vishnu)

Where He Resides: He lies in the Causal Ocean (Karanodaka or Karana Sagar). This ocean sits just outside the spiritual sky of Vaikuntha and acts as the lining of the material world. [3, 9, 10, 11, 12]

His Cosmic Role: He does not enter Vaikuntha or the individual universes. He simply rests on Ananta Shesha in this Causal Ocean. As He exhales, countless bubble-like material universes manifest from the pores of His skin, and when He inhales, they are pulled back into His body. [1, 3, 9, 13]

## 2. Garbhodakasayi Vishnu

Where He Resides: He enters into each and every individual material universe created by Mahavishnu.

His Cosmic Role: He fills the bottom half of each universe with His perspiration (the Garbhodaka Ocean). He rests there, and from His navel sprouts the golden lotus flower upon which Lord Brahma is born to construct the physical planets. [9, 10, 14]

## 3. Kshirodakasayi Vishnu (The Paramatma)

Where He Resides: He lives on a special spiritual island called Shvetadvipa, situated within the Ocean of Milk (Kshira Sagara). He also enters into every single atom and resides directly in the hearts of all living beings. [10, 15, 16, 17, 18]

His Cosmic Role: This is the form of Vishnu that the demigods (like Brahma and Indra) pray to when the material world is in trouble, prompting Him to descend to Earth as avatars like Rama or Krishna. [19, 20, 21, 22]

## Summary of Who Lives Where

The Spiritual Sky (75%): Resided over by Lord Narayana / Krishna in Vaikuntha and Goloka.

The Borderline: Resided over by Mahavishnu in the Causal Ocean.

The Material World (25%): Directed internally by Garbhodakasayi Vishnu (base of the universe) and Kshirodakasayi Vishnu (inside our hearts and atoms). [1, 2, 3, 9, 10, 15]
