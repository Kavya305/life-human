  The Three Viṣṇus: What Does the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam Actually Say?

  The idea of the “Three Viṣṇus” can appear complicated at first.


  Are they three different Gods?

  Are they three separate forms of Viṣṇu?

  Or are they three manifestations of the same Supreme Reality functioning at different levels of creation?


  The Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam gives us a remarkably systematic description of this subject.
