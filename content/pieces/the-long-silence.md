There is a particular quality of thought that only arrives after a long stretch of being unreachable. Not the first hour — the first hour is mostly restlessness. Somewhere past it, something settles, and a person begins to hear what they actually think rather than what they have been repeating.

:::interpretation
Nearly every contemplative tradition builds in an extended, deliberately boring period of withdrawal. The consistency suggests they were protecting access to something that does not arrive quickly.
:::

The modern condition is not that we lack solitude. It is that we have made it almost impossible to enter accidentally. Every interval that used to produce it — the queue, the walk, the wait, the commute, the minutes before sleep — has been filled. Solitude now requires a decision, and decisions are expensive.

:::question
If you have not been bored in a year, what has not had the chance to occur to you?
:::
