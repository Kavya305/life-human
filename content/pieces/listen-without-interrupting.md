Try it once and the difficulty is immediate. Somewhere in the second sentence, your attention leaves the person in front of you and goes to work assembling a response. From there you are no longer listening. You are waiting.

:::interpretation
Most of what passes for conversation is two people taking turns to wait. This is not a moral failure. It is what attention does when it is not held deliberately.
:::

The practice is small enough to actually do. One conversation. Do not plan the reply. Let the silence after they finish be slightly longer than is comfortable. Notice what you hear in it.

:::note
There is nothing to track and nothing to complete. If you do it once and never again, something still happened once — and the person you did it for noticed, even if they could not name what was different.
:::
